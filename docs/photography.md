# Destination photography

These are real photographs downloaded from the photographers’ official Unsplash photo pages. The original eight international photographs were verified on 19 October 2026; the six India photographs were verified on 22 October 2026. Each selected photo page identifies the image as free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial website use and local adaptation. Photographer credit is appreciated, though the license does not require it. None of these selections uses Unsplash+ licensing or an AI illustration.

The files are hosted locally, resized to a maximum edge of 1,600 pixels and encoded as WebP. Next.js further serves responsive image sizes. Destination images illustrate the places described; they do not imply a partnership with the pictured properties.
